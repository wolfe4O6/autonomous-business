# Prior experiments and implications

These are operator publications or reporting, not independently audited profit statements. Revenue, investment, and simulation scores are not interchangeable.

| Experiment | Reported outcome | Design implication |
|---|---|---|
| HustleGPT / Green Gadget Guru (2023) | Human-executed $100 plan; reported donations/investment around $7,700; claimed $130 revenue questioned by reporting; site later defunct | Verify a usable product and actual customer receipts, not attention |
| Project Vend phase one | Office shop lost money; discounts and social manipulation undermined decisions | Expose costs and enforce boundaries |
| Project Vend phase two | Improved business margins with newer models/tools; human help and purchase approval remained; AI CEO not clearly beneficial | Structured tools and evidence beat motivational group chat |
| Bottleneck Labs seven businesses | Operators report $0 outside revenue, $2,833.35 model costs and $359.80 business spend over a 72-hour experiment; spam and unsolicited invoices | Count compute, constrain outreach/invoicing, enforce stop rules |
| Andon Market | Real retail operations with human staff; operator page reports not yet profitable and describes memory/strategic-analysis problems | Operational activity is not profitability; require business reviews |
